The Engagement Ring Industry Relies on Confusion

Most buyers walk into a jeweller armed with a budget but no real understanding of what determines quality. That's exactly how the industry wants it.

Diamond Terminology

The 4Cs sound simple but are widely misunderstood. Clarity grades that sound impressive often make zero visible difference.

Inflated Pricing

Retail jewellers mark up diamonds 2–3x. Most of what you pay covers their shopfront, not your ring.

Lab vs Natural Myths

Lab-grown diamonds are chemically identical to mined. The choice isn't about quality — it's about values and budget.

Misleading Marketing

"Sale" prices, inflated RRPs, and vague certifications are designed to create urgency, not inform your decision.

Before buying an engagement ring, most couples misunderstand what determines quality.

Carat size doesn’t tell you how beautiful a diamond looks. Brand markups don’t reflect better craftsmanship. And lab vs natural isn’t a quality decision — it’s a values decision. Most couples don’t learn this until after they’ve overspent.
