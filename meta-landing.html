FAQ

Questions buyers ask before getting in touch.

Large branded jewellers carry significant overheads: prime retail locations, national advertising campaigns, and brand prestige premiums. These costs are built into the price of every ring you see in their showroom. As a private jeweller operating from a single Sydney location, our overheads are a fraction of theirs. That difference directly benefits your budget — you receive the same or higher quality craftsmanship without paying for someone else's marketing.

In many cases, the quality is higher. Major retailers optimise for volume. Our focus is on individual craftsmanship — band thickness, casting density, prong construction, and setting integrity. Belmir personally oversees every ring. We also back every piece with a lifetime manufacturing warranty, which demonstrates a level of confidence in the work that most volume retailers cannot match.

A properly set diamond sits level in the setting, with prongs of equal length gripping the stone symmetrically at the girdle. During your consultation, Belmir walks you through exactly what correct setting looks like — including visual comparisons. We also explain the stone seat (the notch cut into each prong) and why its precision matters for long-term security. You will know what to look for before you commit to anything.

Quality comes from two things: the diamond and the ring structure. For the diamond, cut is the most important factor — it determines how light returns to the eye and creates brilliance. For the ring, what matters is casting density (no porosity), band thickness suited to daily wear, prong construction, and metal alloy quality. Most buyers only focus on the diamond. We make sure you understand both sides so you can make a genuinely informed decision.

Yes. Both options are available and both are explained honestly during your consultation. Lab-grown and natural diamonds are chemically, optically, and physically identical — the difference is origin. Lab-grown stones are typically 60–80% less expensive for the same size and quality grade, which allows many buyers to significantly improve the cut quality or ring craftsmanship within the same budget. The right choice depends on your values and priorities, and Belmir will help you think through both without pushing either.
