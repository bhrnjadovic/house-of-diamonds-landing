Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, 100%. Lab-grown diamonds are chemically, physically, and optically identical to mined diamonds. They are pure carbon, crystallised in the same cubic structure. The only difference is their origin — grown in a controlled laboratory environment rather than extracted from the earth. They are certified by the same gemological institutes (IGI) and are indistinguishable from mined diamonds.

Lab-grown diamonds skip the expensive mining, transportation, and middlemen involved in mined diamonds. The production process is more efficient and the supply chain is shorter. This means you get the same quality diamond — often with better clarity and colour — at 60-80% less than a comparable mined stone. The savings go into getting a bigger, better diamond or a more elaborate ring design.
